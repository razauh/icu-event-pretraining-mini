# Limitations

- The repository uses only the eICU demo v2.0.1 dataset and does not support
  claims about broader hospital populations.
- The cohort is small and selectively sampled, so results are exploratory.
- The project does not claim clinical deployment readiness, decision support,
  or prospective utility.
- FedAvg is a simulation over grouped local runs, not secure federated learning.
- Public summaries are aggregate only and do not provide privacy guarantees.
- Missing or incompatible checkpoints, run-state files, or optional reports can
  prevent automatic resume until the affected stage is rerun.
